# Staying legal (plain English - not legal advice)

- **CAN-SPAM (email):** one-to-one is fine; use your real name, an honest subject, a way to opt out, and honor opt-outs fast. No bought lists, no mass emailing.
- **TCPA (calls/texts):** do **not** use autodialers, prerecorded/robocall voice, or bulk-text software. Personally typing a text to one business is different, but keep it rare, identify yourself, and stop if told to. Check the Do-Not-Call rules and Wisconsin's telemarketing rules (Wisconsin DATCP) before phoning anyone, or ask a local small-business advisor. Walking in is the safest.
- **Data:** leads come from OpenStreetMap (ODbL license, "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors"). Don't scrape sites whose terms forbid it.
- **Demos:** never present a demo as the business's official site. Demos are labeled "preview" and hidden from Google. Don't use a business's logos/photos you don't have rights to.
- **Contracts:** get a short written OK (a text/email is fine) before going live and before charging. Let clients cancel anytime.
- **Taxes:** track income; consider a simple business registration and ask about Wisconsin sales tax on services/website hosting.
