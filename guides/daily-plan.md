# Your simple daily plan (about 2 hours)

**Each morning (10 min):** open `data/visit-plan.md` (Top 10 with routes) or `data/top20.md`. Tap the **Google** link for each stop: if they already have a real website, mark **not interested** and skip.

**Prep (once or twice a week):** you do NOT need a custom demo for every cold lead. Show a sample design on your phone at the door. Only after an owner shows interest and you finish the intake (`pitch/owner-intake.md`), send the photos + notes to Claude: it writes the design brief, builds their own one-of-a-kind demo, checks it, and gives you the link and a "still need from the owner" list.

**Visit (60-90 min):**
- Walk into **8-10 places**. Use `pitch/walk-in-script.md` (60 seconds each). 
- Immediately after each: set status -> **contacted** (or **not interested**) + a note.

**Follow up (15 min):**
- Day 3-4: one friendly text/DM to "contacted" leads who said "maybe" (`pitch/message-templates.md`). Only once.
- Interested -> collect hours/photos, update site, get their OK, send the payment link, mark **paid**.

**Weekly (Monday):** the phone alert tells you how many new leads arrived. Check Stripe for payments and failed charges.

**Goal for each route (5 stops, 1.5-2 hours):** introduce yourself as being with Cream City Web Co., show their preview, collect the intake (sign photo, hours). 

**Goals:** 10 visits/day -> expect ~1 in 10 interested at first. 1-2 sales a week = ~$250-500 up front plus growing monthly income. Be patient; it's a numbers game and being polite matters.
