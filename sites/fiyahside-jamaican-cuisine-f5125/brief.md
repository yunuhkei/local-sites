# Design brief - Fiyahside Jamaican Cuisine

> Built from public information only (name, address, phone, and a few public directory listings). We have not met the owner or seen the shop yet, so anything marked as a first guess must be confirmed at the visit.

## Customers
People who want jerk chicken, oxtail and other Jamaican dishes and have heard about the place from a friend, the news or a delivery app. They check the menu and the hours before driving over, and they want to know if there is parking.

## Neighborhood
West Capitol Drive near N 74th Street: this is the restaurant's second location, opened in 2023 (the first is on West Villard Avenue). A main road with a small lot.

## Vibe
Warm, loud, flavorful. A neighborhood spot with a following, not a chain.

## References
- A paper takeout menu with a green, gold and black stripe along the top.
- A hand-painted sign above a Caribbean takeout window.
- A paper-lined takeout tray.

## Palette
Warm paper (#FFF4D6), black ink (#15130E), forest green (#0C6B3C) for buttons, and gold (#F2B705) for the stripe band. A first guess: we have not seen their sign.

## Type
Lilita One for headings: bold, rounded and friendly, like a painted menu board. Cabin for body text: warm and readable.

## Layout
Menu board on paper: a bordered menu under a three-color stripe. Order: menu, about, facts, hours, photos, contact. The menu is first and is marked TO GET FROM OWNER for now.

## Signature
The green, gold and black stripe band across the top of the menu.

## Voice
Short and hungry. Use the dish names the owner uses. No claims about being the best.

## TO GET FROM OWNER
- The menu with prices if they want them shown, hours, confirm the phone number, delivery apps, parking, payment types, photos of food and the sign, the story behind the name, whether the other location should be listed.
