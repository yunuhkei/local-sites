# Design brief - Cre8tive Kidz Learning Center

> Built from public information only (name, address, phone, and a few public directory listings). We have not met the owner or seen the shop yet, so anything marked as a first guess must be confirmed at the visit.

## Customers
Parents who need care for children from babies to school-age kids, early mornings to the evening. This is in ZIP 53218, the home neighborhood of the business, so it is close to base. They look up the hours and ages first and expect a phone number they can tap.

## Neighborhood
West Appleton Avenue near N 80th Street, in ZIP 53218, on the same strip as a barbershop, several salons and an auto electrician.

## Vibe
Playful and homemade. The name spells Creative with the number 8, so a little wit is part of the brand.

## References
- A striped, scalloped awning over a neighborhood storefront.
- Crayon labels on a cubby.
- A hand-lettered sign in a daycare front window.

## Palette
Coral (#C8431B) and teal-green (#1A7F6B) for the awning stripes, on a warm-white page (#FFFAF0), with deep ink (#1D2B36) for text. A first guess: we have not seen the sign. Both colors pass contrast with white lettering.

## Type
Fredoka for headings: round, bold and playful, like cut-out letters. Mulish for body text: plain and calm so parents can read quickly.

## Layout
Storefront: a striped awning with a scalloped edge over a rounded sign carrying the name stacked in four short lines. Order: facts (ages), hours, services, about, photos, contact. Parents care about ages and hours first.

## Signature
The scalloped striped awning across the top, like the awning on the actual storefront.

## Voice
Cheerful and plain. Say the ages and the hours. No promises about outcomes.

## TO GET FROM OWNER
- Hours (two sources disagree), confirm the license and ages, program details, meals, enrollment steps, photos (no children's faces without parent permission), the sign colors.
