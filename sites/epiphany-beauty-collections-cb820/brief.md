# Design brief - Epiphany Beauty Collections

> Built from public information only (name, address, phone, and a few public directory listings). We have not met the owner or seen the shop yet, so anything marked as a first guess must be confirmed at the visit.

## Customers
Women booking a wig install, a sew-in or extensions, often for an event or a big change, who want to see exactly what the shop does before calling. They tend to follow shops on social media and then look for a phone number and an address.

## Neighborhood
West Capitol Drive near N 62nd Street, Suite 14 in a strip of small shops. People may have to look for the suite number, so it is on the page.

## Vibe
Glamorous but personal. A boutique, not a chain. Confident, a little dramatic.

## References
- A satin-lined wig box in black with rose-gold lettering.
- The cover of a fashion magazine: one big name, a thin rule, numbered features inside.
- A beauty-supply store window at night, lit from inside.

## Palette
Near-black (#161011) for the whole page, rose gold (#E3A585) for rules, numerals and buttons, and warm blush-white (#F4E8E0) for text. A first guess: we have not seen their sign or brand colors.

## Type
DM Serif Display for headings: an elegant display serif with glamour, set big like a magazine cover. Manrope for body text: modern and legible on a dark background.

## Layout
Editorial cover: the name huge, a rose-gold rule on the left, sections numbered 01, 02, 03 like a table of contents. Order: services, about, hours, facts, photos, contact.

## Signature
Oversized rose-gold section numerals, one per section.

## Voice
Confident and short. Name the services the way customers ask for them.

## TO GET FROM OWNER
- Hours, the right phone number, confirm the services (from a directory), booking by appointment or walk-in, price ranges if they want them shown, photos of finished work with permission, Facebook page.
