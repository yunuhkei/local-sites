# Design brief - Nuno's Auto Body

> Built from public information only (name, address, phone, and a few public directory listings). We have not met the owner or seen the shop yet, so anything marked as a first guess must be confirmed at the visit.

## Customers
Drivers after a fender-bender or a scrape, often sent by an insurance company or a friend. They want to see that a shop does body work, how to reach it, and when it is open. They are comparing a few shops, and the one that answers its phone wins.

## Neighborhood
West Fond du Lac Avenue near N 92nd Street, next door to another auto shop: a block where people already come to get cars fixed.

## Vibe
Workshop, practical, color-obsessed. A body shop is judged by how good the finish looks.

## References
- A fan of paint-chip cards from an auto-paint supplier.
- Primer-grey panels on a car waiting for color.
- A black shop jacket with the shop name stitched in white.

## Palette
Primer grey (#F3F3F0) for the page, near-black (#101214) for the hero and text, and one paint blue (#1F5FD6) for buttons and the swatch row. A first guess: we have not seen their sign. A body shop is about color, so we use a single strong one.

## Type
Archivo Black for headings: a heavy, wide grotesk like the lettering on a body-shop truck. DM Sans for body text: clean and modern.

## Layout
Blocks: stacked color panels, each its own shape, with square swatch strips for the services. Order: services, about, hours, photos, facts, contact. Services first because body work is the whole question.

## Signature
A row of four paint-chip swatches under the name, getting lighter, like a color card.

## Voice
Short and plain. Say what they repair. No promises about insurance or timelines.

## TO GET FROM OWNER
- Services (collision, dents, paint?), hours, what insurance they work with, years in business, photos of finished work and the sign, payment types, languages spoken.
