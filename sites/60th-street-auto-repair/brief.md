# Design brief - 60th Street Auto Repair (SAMPLE, a pretend auto shop)

> This is a sample to show the system. The business, phone and address are made up. A real brief is written after meeting the owner.

## Customers
People who drive to work and cannot be without the car: a warning light came on, the brakes are squealing, the oil is overdue. They are stressed, they are probably calling from a parking lot, and they want a straight answer and a time. Many search "auto repair near me" on a phone, then call the first shop that looks honest.

## Neighborhood
Northwest Milwaukee, 53218: busy through-streets, bus lines, a shop on a main road with a lot you can pull into. People know the shop by the cross street.

## Vibe
Working garage. Clean, direct, a little gruff in a good way. Like the printed work order you get handed with your keys.

## References
- A carbon-copy work order: numbered lines, ruled rows, stamped total.
- The sticker a shop puts on the windshield after an oil change: bold header bar, boxes, tear-off edge.
- Yellow-and-charcoal shop signage and the painted lines on a garage floor.

## Palette
Safety yellow (#F2C200) from the painted floor lines and the sign. Charcoal (#1C1E21) from the lift, the tools and the tires. Warm shop-paper grey (#F2F1EC) from the work-order pad. Yellow is a fill with dark text on it, never small text on grey.

## Type
Barlow Condensed for headings: a condensed grotesk, like the stenciled and stamped lettering on shop signs, so long names like "60th Street Auto Repair" fit large on a phone. IBM Plex Sans for body: engineered, sturdy, easy to read.

## Layout
"Split screen": on a phone the charcoal panel (name, one-line promise, big yellow Call button) is on top and the work-order list follows. On desktop the panel stays fixed on the left while the right side scrolls. Order: services, hours, good-to-know, photos, about, contact. A person with a broken car wants "do you fix this?" before anything else.

## Signature
The hours are the windshield sticker: charcoal header bar, white body, dotted rows, tear-off dashed bottom. Today's row turns yellow.

## Voice
Plain and specific, like a mechanic talking at the counter. Name the jobs. No promises about price or speed unless the owner gives them.

## TO GET FROM OWNER
- Opening hours, photos, what the shop is known for, makes they work on, appointments or drop-ins, payment types, languages spoken.
