# Design brief - Trans-X-Press Transmissions

> Built from public information only (name, address, phone, and a few public directory listings). We have not met the owner or seen the shop yet, so anything marked as a first guess must be confirmed at the visit.

## Customers
Drivers whose car will not shift right or will not go into gear. They need a straight answer fast, they may be calling from the side of the road, and they usually need the car back soon. They want to know three things: do you fix this, when are you open, and where are you.

## Neighborhood
West Capitol Drive near N 68th Street: a busy main road, with a small lot and a storefront shop. People find it by the cross street.

## Vibe
Specialist shop. Blunt and practical, like a mechanic explaining a problem at the counter.

## References
- A hand-written work order clipped to a windshield.
- A steel transmission housing on a workshop bench.
- The orange warning label on a bottle of transmission fluid.

## Palette
Steel blue (#1F3A4D) for the big panel, from transmission housings and shop steel. Pale grey (#EEF1F2) for the page, like shop paper. Orange (#FF7A2F) only for the Call button and today's row, the way a warning label uses it. A first guess: we have not seen the real sign.

## Type
Anton for headings: a tall condensed grotesk like stamped lettering on parts and work orders, which lets a long word like Transmissions run big on a phone. Source Sans 3 for body text: plain and hard-working.

## Layout
Split screen with the steel panel on the right on desktop (the Call button is still first on a phone). Order: hours, services, facts, about, photos, contact. Hours come first because a stressed customer needs to know if the shop is open right now.

## Signature
The hours are a windshield sticker: dark header bar, white body, dotted rows, tear-off bottom edge. Today's row turns orange.

## Voice
Mechanic at the counter. Say what they fix. Do not promise prices or turnaround times.

## TO GET FROM OWNER
- Which of two phone numbers is correct. Confirm hours and services (hours shown are from OpenStreetMap and match one directory). Photos, what makes them different, makes they work on, payment types.
