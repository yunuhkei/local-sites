# Design brief - No. 1 Chinese Restaurant

> Built from public information only (name, address, phone, and a few public directory listings). We have not met the owner or seen the shop yet, so anything marked as a first guess must be confirmed at the visit.

## Customers
People ordering dinner on the way home or from the couch. They want the phone number first, the hours second and the menu third. Many will call in an order, so a tap-to-call button is the whole job.

## Neighborhood
West Appleton Avenue near N 75th Street, on the same strip as several salons. A takeout counter, not a sit-down place, according to one directory.

## Vibe
Fast, busy, honest. A takeout counter where the menu is on the wall.

## References
- A lit-up menu board over a takeout counter.
- The red-and-gold paper takeout menu folded into a bag.
- A neon OPEN sign glowing in a window after dark.

## Palette
Deep lacquer red (#7A1712) for the board, near-black (#1B0D0A) for the page behind it, gold (#F2B705) for the neon OPEN sign and buttons, and cream (#FBEFD5) for text. A first guess: we have not seen their sign.

## Type
Teko for headings: tall, tight capitals like painted menu-board lettering, with a clear numeral 1 (the first font tried drew it like a capital I). Hind for body text: simple and readable at a glance.

## Layout
Menu board: the whole page is a framed board in the middle of the screen. Order: menu, facts, hours, about, photos, contact. The menu is first because it is what a hungry person came for, and it is currently marked TO GET FROM OWNER.

## Signature
The hours are a neon-style OPEN sign: gold outline, a slight glow on the heading.

## Voice
Short lines. Menu words, not marketing words.

## TO GET FROM OWNER
- The menu (a photo of the printed menu works) with prices if they want them shown, confirm take-out only, confirm hours (a day is listed as closed), delivery apps, payment types, Facebook page, photos.
