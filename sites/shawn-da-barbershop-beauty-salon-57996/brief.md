# Design brief - Shawn Da' Barbershop & Beauty Salon

> Built from public information only (name, address, phone, and a few public directory listings). We have not met the owner or seen the shop yet, so anything marked as a first guess must be confirmed at the visit.

## Customers
Men and boys who want a cut or a shave, and people who come for the beauty side of the shop: one address, two chairs-and-stations businesses under one name. Regulars who know exactly who they sit with. They check hours on their phone before they walk over because the schedule is not the same every day.

## Neighborhood
West Appleton Avenue near N 75th Street on the northwest side: a stretch of small storefronts, the same strip as several other salons and a restaurant. Foot traffic and cars both.

## Vibe
Busy, confident neighborhood shop. Open long days. Plain and proud, not fancy.

## References
- The gold-lettered black window sign you see on classic barbershops.
- A price board in a barbershop: services in big letters, nothing else.
- A black leather barber chair under a bright shop light.

## Palette
Black (#15130F) and warm gold (#D9A83C) with a bone-white (#F3EDE0) text color. A first guess: we have not seen their sign yet, so this follows the oldest barbershop color pairing. The gold is used as one wide band so the name is readable from across the street.

## Type
Bebas Neue for headings: tall, condensed capitals like sign-painter lettering, so a long name like Shawn Da' Barbershop & Beauty Salon fits large on a phone. Karla for body text: friendly and clear.

## Layout
Poster with the name set on a full-width gold band, so the whole name is the sign. Order: services, hours, about, photos, facts, contact. The services come first because there are two businesses under one name and people want to know the shop does their thing.

## Signature
The hours are a double-ruled gold card, because this shop's hours are unusual (long weekdays, short Monday and Sunday) and need to be easy to find.

## Voice
Short and direct. Name the services plainly. No claims about being the best.

## TO GET FROM OWNER
- Confirm the services, the hours, and the Facebook page. A photo of the sign and the chairs. Who owns the shop and how they describe themselves.
