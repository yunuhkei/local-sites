# Design brief - Mussomelli's Auto Care

> Built from public information only (name, address, phone, and a few public directory listings). We have not met the owner or seen the shop yet, so anything marked as a first guess must be confirmed at the visit.

## Customers
Neighbors with a car that needs looking after: oil, brakes, a noise they cannot name. They want a shop they can trust with the keys. A family surname on the sign means word of mouth matters more than anything online, but they still look up the hours and the phone number first.

## Neighborhood
West Fond du Lac Avenue near N 91st Street, on the northwest edge of Milwaukee, close to the city line: a long main road with a mix of small shops.

## Vibe
Old-school family garage. Plain-spoken, steady, a little formal in the way a surname on the sign is.

## References
- A hand-painted wooden shop sign in dark green with cream letters.
- The painted pane of glass in a shop door that lists the hours.
- A parchment-colored service invoice with the shop's name across the top.

## Palette
Parchment (#F2EBD8) from old invoices and wood-sign paint. Deep forest green (#1F3A2A for the sign board, #2F6B3F for buttons). Ink (#1D2B21) for text. A first guess: we have not seen their real sign.

## Type
Patua One for headings: a sturdy slab with soft corners that looks like lettering painted on wood. Libre Franklin for body text: honest and neutral, easy on the eyes.

## Layout
Storefront: the name on a dark sign board at the top, like the board over the door, with the hours in a painted-window pane below. Order: hours, about, services, facts, photos, contact. We have very little verified information about this shop, so the page is short on purpose and labels what is missing.

## Signature
The hours sit in a painted window pane with a double ruled border and big painted lettering, like the OPEN hours on a shop door.

## Voice
Few words. Surname-on-the-sign dignity. Never claim years in business until the owner says so.

## TO GET FROM OWNER
- Everything beyond the name, phone and address: hours, services, years in business, who the family is, photos of the sign and the shop, payment types, languages spoken.
