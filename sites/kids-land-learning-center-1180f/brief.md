# Design brief - Kids Land Learning Center

> Built from public information only (name, address, phone, and a few public directory listings). We have not met the owner or seen the shop yet, so anything marked as a first guess must be confirmed at the visit.

## Customers
Parents who need dependable care while they work, and who check the hours first because drop-off at 6am and pick-up near 9pm are the whole question. They want to know it is licensed, what ages it takes, and how to call. They are often reading on a phone in a parking lot.

## Neighborhood
West Capitol Drive near N 79th Street on the northwest side: a main road, a daycare that parents drive to on the way to work.

## Vibe
Warm, organized, trustworthy. A place a parent would feel good leaving their child. Cheerful without being babyish.

## References
- A crayon box: a few strong colors, nothing fancy.
- A school bulletin board with big rounded letters.
- A pick-up and drop-off sign-in sheet on a clipboard.

## Palette
Sunshine yellow (#F5B700) for the big hero tab and buttons, ink navy (#1B2A49) for text and the dark panels, and a warm cream (#FFF7DB) page. A first guess: we have not seen their sign.

## Type
Baloo 2 for headings: round, friendly letters that are easy for a kid to read, but sturdy enough to look professional. Nunito for body text: soft and very readable.

## Layout
Blocks: stacked rounded color panels, each its own shape, so the page feels like a bulletin board. Order: about, facts, hours, services, photos, contact. Parents scan for hours and ages first.

## Signature
The hero is a sun-yellow tab with one big rounded corner.

## Voice
Calm and clear. Say what is true: licensed, ages, hours. No promises about education outcomes.

## TO GET FROM OWNER
- Confirm the license and the program details (from public directories). The right phone number. Confirm hours (OpenStreetMap lists Mon-Fri 6am-9pm). Ages served, capacity, meals, enrollment steps, photos (no children's faces without parent permission), Facebook page.
