# Owner intake (2 minutes, on your phone's Notes app)

Ask these while you're standing there. They feed the design brief, so the site fits THEIR shop and nothing is made up. Send the answers to Claude with the photos.

**Look (take photos of these):**
1. Their sign - what colors and lettering? (photo, straight-on, daytime)
2. Storefront and inside, wide shot
3. Owner/team at work (ask permission)
4. Their best work or best dish, close up
5. Anything hand-painted or unique to them

**Facts (write exactly what they say):**
- Business name spelled exactly, phone to show, address, email (optional)
- Hours, every day (and holidays closed)
- What are you known for? How long open? Who are your regulars?
- Walk-ins or appointments? Parking? Payment types? Languages spoken?
- Services/menu with prices *only if they want prices shown*
- Facebook / Instagram links
- "Is there anything you'd NOT want on the site?"

**Close:** "I'll text you the preview link. Nothing goes live until you say OK."
