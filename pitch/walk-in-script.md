# Walk-in script (about 60 seconds) - Cream City Web Co.

**Who you are:** you speak for the company, not as a named person. Say "I'm with Cream City Web Co." If someone asks your name, use the first name you want to use with customers (any workers you hire later can use their own); contracts and payments are always with **Cream City Web Co.** (see `guides/legal-basics.md`).

**Best times:** barbers and salons Tue-Thu mid-morning or early afternoon; restaurants 2-4pm (between rushes); auto shops and daycares Tue-Thu 9:30-11am. Avoid rushes. Dress neat, smile. Have their demo link open on your phone, plus a few printed price sheets (`docs/price-sheet`) and cards.

**Before you go in:** tap the Google check link in `data/visit-plan.md`. If they already have a real website, mark **not interested** and skip.

**1. Open (ask the person at the counter)**
> "Hi! I'm with Cream City Web Co. We make websites for local Milwaukee shops. Is the owner or manager around for 30 seconds? I'm not selling anything today, I just want to show them something."

If the owner isn't there: leave a card and a price sheet, ask "When's a good time to catch them?", write it down, mark the lead **contacted**. Done.

**2. Hook (owner comes over)**
> "I looked for [Business] online and couldn't find a website of your own, just [what you found: a directory listing / a Facebook page]. So we made a preview of what yours could look like. Can I show you on my phone?"

**3. Show their preview** (the demo link in the visit plan). Let them scroll. Point out: the Call button, directions, hours. Say: "This uses what we found online. Tell me what's wrong or missing and we'll fix it. Your real sign colors and photos would go in too."

**3b. Quick intake if they're interested:** use `pitch/owner-intake.md`. Take a photo of the sign (for their colors), the shop, and write down their hours exactly as they say them.

**4. Price, plainly**
> "It's $500 for the site, split in two: $250 to get started once you've said yes, and the other $250 when it goes live. Then it's $25 a month for hosting and any small changes. The site is one page, and each extra page is $100. You pay nothing until you've seen your preview and said yes, and nothing goes live until you say OK."

**5. Ask**
> "Want us to fix up the details and send you the final version this week?"

**If they say:**
- *"I have Facebook."* "Totally, keep it. A website is what shows up on Google with your hours, phone and map in one tap. We'll link your Facebook from it."
- *"Too expensive."* "I get it. It's split into two payments, and the monthly part is under a dollar a day."
- *"Not interested."* "No problem, thanks for your time!" Mark **not interested** and don't come back to pitch again.
- *"Let me think."* "Of course. Can I text you the link so you can look later?" (Get their OK to text first. See message templates.)

**Rules:** be honest, never pressure, never say the site is live or official, never take the $250 deposit before they've seen their preview and said yes, and send the other $250 link only when the site goes live. Leave a card: "Cream City Web Co."
