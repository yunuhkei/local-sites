# Message templates (one-to-one only) - from Joaquin, Cream City Web Co.

Send these **yourself, one at a time, to a business's public number or DM**, ideally after you've spoken or visited. Never use bulk-texting tools, autodialers, robocalls or mass email. Replace `[Owner]`, `[Business]`, `[DEMO LINK]` and `[my number]`.

## Text after a visit (with their OK)
> Hi [Owner], it's Joaquin from Cream City Web Co., I stopped by [Business] today. Here's the preview I mentioned: [DEMO LINK]. Tell me what you'd change and I'll update it. No pressure. Reply STOP and I won't message again.

## First message if you couldn't visit (send once, no repeats)
> Hi, I'm Joaquin, a local Milwaukee web designer (Cream City Web Co.). I couldn't find a website for [Business], so I made a free preview: [DEMO LINK]. If you like it, it's $250 to set up and $25 a month, and nothing goes live until you say so. If it's not for you, just tell me and I won't message again.

## Follow-up (once, 3-4 days later; then stop)
> Hi [Owner], Joaquin again. Just checking you saw the preview for [Business]: [DEMO LINK]. Happy to change anything. If it's not for you, just say so and I'll close it out.

## They say yes
> Awesome! Send me your hours, services (and prices if you want them shown), and a few photos, or I can take them. I'll have it ready in a few days. When you've approved it, I'll send a secure payment link. Nothing goes live until you OK it.

## Rules of thumb
- Message from your own phone or number. Say who you are (Joaquin, Cream City Web Co.). Give an easy opt-out and honor it immediately.
- One follow-up max. "Not interested" = mark it in the tracker and never contact again.
- Prefer walking in or calling the business line. Don't text a number that looks like someone's personal cell if you can avoid it.
- Email: only one-to-one, real name and address, honest subject, an opt-out line (CAN-SPAM). No bought lists.
