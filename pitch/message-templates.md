# Message templates (one-to-one only)

Send these **yourself, one at a time, to a business's public number or DM**, ideally only after you've spoken/visited or when a walk-in isn't possible. Never use bulk-texting tools, autodialers, robocalls or mass email.

## Text (after a visit, with their OK)
> Hi [Owner], it's [Name] who stopped by [Business] today. Here's the website preview I mentioned: [DEMO LINK]. No pressure - tell me what you'd change and I'll update it. Reply STOP and I won't message again.

## Cold text/DM (no visit yet - send once, no repeats)
> Hi, I'm [Name], a local Milwaukee web designer. I made a free preview website for [Business] since I couldn't find one online: [DEMO LINK]. If you like it, I can finish it for $250 + $25/mo. If not, no worries - just say so and I'll never message again.

## Follow-up (once, 3-4 days later; then stop)
> Hi [Owner], just checking you saw the preview for [Business]: [DEMO LINK]. Happy to change anything. If it's not for you, just tell me and I'll close it out!

## They say yes
> Awesome! Send me your hours, services/prices, and a few photos (or I can take some). I'll have it ready in [2-3] days. Once you approve it, I'll send a secure payment link. Nothing goes live until you OK it.

## Rules of thumb
- Message from your own phone/number; say who you are; give an easy opt-out; honor it immediately.
- One follow-up max. "Not interested" = mark it in the tracker and never contact again.
- Don't text numbers that look like someone's personal cell if you can avoid it - prefer walking in or calling the business line.
- Email: only one-to-one, real name/address, honest subject, and an opt-out line (CAN-SPAM). No purchased lists.
