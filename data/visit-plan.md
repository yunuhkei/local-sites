# Visit plan

Two short routes, each about 1.5-2 hours with 5 stops. **Before each stop: tap the Google check link, mark the lead *contacted* afterward, and read `pitch/walk-in-script.md`.** Demo links go live when the pull request is merged and Pages is on. Phone numbers come from OpenStreetMap; where directories disagree it says so.


## Route 1: West loop (Fond du Lac, then Appleton and Capitol near 80th)

**When:** Tuesday to Thursday, 9:30am to 12:00pm (auto shops, child care and nails are best after the morning rush)  
**Distance between stops:** about 4.0 miles (5 stops), start wherever you are  
**Google Maps route:** [Open the route](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&travelmode=driving&destination=43.0905078,-88.0093684&waypoints=43.1284042,-88.0262335|43.1295341,-88.027429|43.1010892,-88.0126356|43.0901761,-88.01136)

  - "I looked you up and the only place I could find you was a BBB listing, with no hours or website. I made a preview so neighbors can find your phone number and address in one tap. Give me your hours and I'll add them."
