Questions

How long does it take?

The free preview is usually ready within a few days of when we talk. Once you say yes, your site is live within a week.

Do I own it?

Your business name, words, photos and domain name are yours. If you buy a domain, it is registered in your name. If you ever stop the monthly plan, I'll hand over your site files.

What is the $25 a month for?

Keeping your site online and secure, plus small updates: new hours, a new price, a new photo. Text me and it's done.

Can I update it myself or ask you to?

Just ask. Text or call me what to change and I'll do it.

What if I already have a Facebook page?

Keep it. Your site can link to it. Facebook is good for posts, and a website is what people find on Google with your hours, phone number and map in one tap.

What do I pay, and when?

Nothing until you've seen your preview and said yes. Then the site is $500, paid in two parts: $250 to get started, and the other $250 when your site goes live. The $25 a month starts when it goes live.
