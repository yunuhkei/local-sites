# Rules for every website built in this repo (from the owner - follow every time)

1. **Brief first.** Before code, write `sites/<slug>/brief.md` (template: `sitemaker/BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md`): customers, neighborhood, vibe, 3 real-world references, palette (and why), type, layout, signature element. Every design choice traces back to it. `make_site.py` refuses to build without it.
2. **Every site different.** `design-log.json` records layout, font pair, palette, hero, section order. A new site may share at most ONE of those with any earlier site (enforced). Characterful Google font pair (never Inter/Roboto/Poppins for headings). Palette from the business itself, 2-3 colors + neutrals, reason written down. One signature element per site. New business that fits no layout -> write a new layout in `sitemaker/layouts/<name>/`.
3. **Banned "AI look":** purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism, glowing blobs, 3D shapes, generic hero art, emoji icons, sparkles, three identical icon cards, centered "Welcome to X" over blurry stock, fake testimonials/ratings/awards/"trusted by", made-up prices or hours, stock photos of models/handshakes/laptops, and the words: elevate, seamless, unlock, discover, experience the difference, passion, journey, cutting-edge, world-class, one-stop shop, look no further. Missing info = visible `TO GET FROM OWNER` + an entry in `sites/<slug>/TO-GET-FROM-OWNER.md`, never invented.
4. **Write like a local business:** short, specific, plain, owner's voice; real street/neighborhood/details. A headline that works for any other shop gets rewritten.
5. **Done means:** works at 375px (no sideways scroll, 44px+ tap targets); tap-to-call, Maps link, hours with today highlighted, main button visible without scrolling; no `#` links/lorem/placeholders except labeled TO GET items; unique title+description, LocalBusiness JSON-LD, Open Graph image, favicon, 404 page; WCAG AA contrast, alt text, heading order, keyboard focus; no frameworks, compressed images, Lighthouse 90+ everywhere (demos show SEO 60 only because they are deliberately `noindex`); no console errors.
6. **Check like a designer:** run `python3 sitemaker/qa.py` (static + Playwright at 375/768/1280, screenshots), LOOK at the screenshots, fix generic/cramped/off-brand, compare with other sites in the log (siblings -> redesign one). Send the owner the phone screenshot, the demo link and the TO GET FROM OWNER list.

7. **Footer on every site:** "Website by Cream City Web Co." linking to our own site (set in `config.json`, rendered by `make_site.py`).
8. **QA also checks** real rendered text contrast (including transparency), footer not hidden by the sticky bar, tap targets, links, console errors. Our own pages are `sitemaker/build_home.py` (config in `config.json`).

Also: never publish a demo as official (`--live` only with the owner's OK and zero blockers); never put secrets in code or chat; one-to-one contact only (see guides/legal-basics.md).
